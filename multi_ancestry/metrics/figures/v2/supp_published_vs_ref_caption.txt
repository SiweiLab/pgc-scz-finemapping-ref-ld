Supplementary Figure 6 | Published in-sample merged-LD fine-mapping against the benchmark.
Published FINEMAP (to cite: Trubetskoy et al. 2022) and FINEMAP and SuSiE-RSS (to cite: Mawson et al. 2026) analyses of the PGC3-SCZ trans-ancestry meta-analysis with merged in-sample LD (dashed lines, hatched bars), compared with our FINEMAP and SuSiE-RSS with merged reference LD (solid), all with at most five causal variants. Grey, the ancestry-aware methods from Fig. 1. a, Precision–recall on the 193 loci, as in Fig. 1b, scored against all 389 positives because the published results list credible-set members only. b, Exact top-variant recovery on the 122 loci, as in Fig. 1c, using each method's own credible sets; grey band, the ancestry-aware range (83–92%). Scoring details and unmatched settings are given in Methods.
