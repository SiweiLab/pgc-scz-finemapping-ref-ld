Supplementary Figure 5 | Changing one variable at a time: published in-sample analyses as the benchmark.
Exact recovery of each benchmark's top variant at its single-signal loci (one credible set, top PIP > 0.1). Loci whose benchmark top variant is strand-ambiguous (A/T or C/G) are excluded: the published merged-LD analyses retain these variants, whereas our analyses, like the SuSiEx benchmark, exclude them, so their inclusion would change the variant set as well as the LD source. A top variant absent from an analysis's input for any other reason counts as not recovered, as in Fig. 1c. Every analysis uses its own purity filter, as in Supplementary Fig. 6b. Our merged-LD analyses (FINEMAP with at most five causal variants; SuSiE-RSS with L = 5) are scored at the benchmark's credible-set coverage: 99% against the SuSiEx benchmark and 95% against the published merged-LD analyses. Changing coverage from 99% to 95% changed no SuSiE-RSS PIP or FINEMAP credible-set count. Hatched, published analyses with in-sample LD; solid, our analyses with reference LD. a, Benchmark: the published PGC3 FINEMAP analysis (108 loci). b, Each published analysis as the benchmark (rows; n, loci scored) against every analysis (columns).
